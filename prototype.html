Detections and proof

Each alert is detected, tracked, sealed and signed by the node.

    Evidence integrity

    Every alert and all clear is sealed at the node. Each check covers the latest 8 anchored roots.

    Why it matters: if anyone edits a record after the fact, its hash changes, the root stops matching, and the check fails. Alerts stay usable as evidence.

    Checks also run automatically every ten seconds.

    Heartbeat integrity

    Nodes sign an all clear every two seconds. A silent node could be blinded or fed a looped video, so silence is treated as an attack.
